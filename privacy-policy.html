Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

At Enerplus Resources, accessible from our corporate portal, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and stakeholders. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Enerplus Resources and how we utilize it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our legal compliance team.

1. Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your personal information.

  • Direct Correspondence: If you contact us directly through land/mineral owner portals or vendor setups, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, and properties details.
  • Account Credentials: When registering for shareholder disclosures or employment alerts, we collect contact credentials to safely deliver verified reporting data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website utilities and portals.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website user experiences.
  • Understand and analyze how you interact with our website elements.
  • Communicate with you regarding investor relations update modules, operational updates, and safety alerts.
  • Send essential operational emails or compliance updates.
  • Find and prevent fraudulent activities or unauthorized dashboard entry breaches.

3. Log Files & Analytics Tracking

Enerplus Resources follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

4. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, including SSL encryptions on critical shareholder links, we cannot guarantee its absolute safety.

5. Data Protection Rights

We want to ensure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held on our systems.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain compliance conditions.
